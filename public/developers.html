How it works

From first request to live in weeks, not months

You don't need to read any documentation to begin. Our integrations desk guides you from the first form to your first live transaction.

  1. 1

    Complete the setup

    Tell us about your organisation, what you want to build and who to contact. It takes about five minutes.

  2. 2

    Get sandbox access

    Within two working days you receive a sandbox key and a welcome call. The sandbox never moves real money.

  3. 3

    Build and test

    Your engineers build against the sandbox with our technical reference and a named contact on our side.

  4. 4

    Go live

    After a short review we switch you to a live key. Keys are rotated every 90 days and can be revoked instantly.

Security & compliance

Built for regulated partners

  • Encrypted connections only, with separate sandbox and live environments.
  • Access keys are shown once, stored securely and limited to the permissions you agree with us.
  • Read and write permissions are issued separately, so oversight bodies only ever see data.
  • Customer details are only shared with authenticated partners, and processing follows the NDPR.
  • Every request is traceable, which makes reconciliation and audits straightforward.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a developer to apply?

No. Anyone in your organisation can complete the setup. We ask for a technical contact so we can send the sandbox details to the right person.

How much does it cost?

Sandbox access is free. Commercial terms for live access depend on your volume and use case, and we agree them with you before go-live.

How long does it take to go live?

Most partners go live within two to six weeks, depending on how much of the API they use and their own internal approvals.

Where can my engineers find the technical details?

Once you have completed the setup, your technical contact receives the full reference. Engineers who want an early look can read the technical reference.
