Questions

Good to know

Why only 50?

The Founding 50 is our first batch. Keeping it to 50 homes lets one dedicated crew install and support every system properly before we expand.

I live abroad. How do I know it was installed?

You name the home in Nigeria and the family contact. We send photos and a video at handover, and you get access to the live meter so you can see the system working.

How does lease-to-own work?

You pay a 30% deposit now. The balance is spread over 24 months under a written agreement you sign before installation. The meter shows your payments, and the system is yours at the end.

What if I change my mind?

Can I pay by bank transfer?

Yes. Choose a naira plan and you will see ENA Plus Ltd's bank details and your payment reference.
