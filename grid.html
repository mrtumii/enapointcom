What we do

Design, build, meter, operate

  1. Measure the load

    Not a survey estimate — metered, over a fortnight, including the peaks nobody mentions.

  2. Design generation and storage

    PV, storage and where necessary a gas or diesel set, sized against the measured curve rather than the nameplate.

  3. Build and meter

    Every connection is a prepaid ENA meter from day one, so revenue collection is not a later problem.

  4. Operate or hand over

    ENA runs it, or trains your team and hands it over. Either way the control platform and the API stay the same.

Utility-scale plant

Beyond the mini-grid scale, ENA develops 100 MW-class solar plant with the grid-evacuation kit — transformers, switchgear and protection — supplied alongside the array rather than left to a separate package that arrives late.

Already running a plant?

Bring existing generation onto ENA metering, control and billing without replacing what produces the power. The connection happens through the developer console.
